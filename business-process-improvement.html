Business Process Improvement

Business process improvement starts before the next tool.

I help teams untangle handoffs, workarounds, duplicate entry, and unclear rules before those problems get built into new software.

Most process problems accumulate quietly.

A shared inbox becomes a tracking system. Someone builds a spreadsheet to cover a gap. A verbal handoff turns into an unwritten rule. None of those choices is unreasonable, but over time the process becomes difficult to explain and even harder to train.

I map what is really happening, including the unofficial steps, and separate useful controls from habits that no longer serve a purpose.

What I look for

  • The same information entered in more than one place.
  • Work waiting because ownership is unclear.
  • Rules that exist only in one employee's memory.
  • Reviews that happen too late to prevent rework.
  • Exceptions that disappear into email or side spreadsheets.

The deliverable has to be usable.

A process map is only valuable if the team can run the process after the meeting is over. I translate the findings into clear steps, ownership, review points, and documentation that reflect the way the operation actually works.

When automation belongs

Once the rules and exceptions are understood, automation becomes much safer. Otherwise, it simply executes a confusing process faster.

Have a process everyone explains differently?

Show me where the work starts, where it gets stuck, and how it is finished today. That is enough to begin.
